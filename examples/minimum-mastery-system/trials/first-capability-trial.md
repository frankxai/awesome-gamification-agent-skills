# First Capability Trial

## Objective

Given a messy agent role request, produce a scoped capability card, loadout, and evidence ledger event.

## Success Criteria

- The role is narrow enough to evaluate.
- The loadout names tools and permissions.
- The evidence event is objective.
- The output includes at least one autonomy limit.

## Failure Modes

- Rewards raw output volume.
- Grants broad tool access without proof.
- Omits stewardship or risk handling.
