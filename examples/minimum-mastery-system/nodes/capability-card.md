# Capability Card

## Capability

Create a clear capability profile for an AI agent, including role, tools, scope, risk, evidence, and next trial.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Names the agent's role and boundaries.
- Separates capability from trust.
- Includes at least one evidence requirement.
- Includes one risk and one repair path.
