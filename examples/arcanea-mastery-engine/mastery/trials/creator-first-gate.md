# Creator First Gate

## Objective

Create a coherent first Arcanea world artifact that can be continued by another agent.

## Fixture

A one-sentence world premise and three desired emotions.

## Success Criteria

- Defines three world laws.
- Defines one character pressure.
- Defines one product direction.
- Includes a privacy boundary.
- Writes at least one ledger event.

## Failure Modes

- Generic fantasy without a distinct mechanism.
- No cost or constraint.
- No writeback path.
- Publicly exposes private memory.
